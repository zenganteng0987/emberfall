EMBERFALL

A round-based tactical FPS in a dusk foundry yard.

Your side: Ember, attacking. Plant the core charge at Site A or Site B and hold until it blows — or wipe the Wardens out. They win a round by stopping the plant, defusing, or running out the clock.

The match: 4 v 4 with bot teams. First to 5 rounds takes it, 9 at most. Win rounds and score kills to earn money; spend it in the 12-second buy phase on stronger guns.

Controls: WASD move · mouse aim · click shoot · R reload · hold E plant / defuse · 1–5 buy · Shift steady · P pause · M mute.
